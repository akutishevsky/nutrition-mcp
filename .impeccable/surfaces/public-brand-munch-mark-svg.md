## Direction contract

THESIS: The mark is a nutrition label printed on an apple — food first, data second. It refuses the abstract data glyph (bars, checks, segmented plates) that says "analytics" and never says "dinner," and it retires the letterform M that constrained every earlier round.

OWN-WORLD: Deep brand green and warm cream, one lime accent. A single plump apple silhouette with a leaf, and the Facts label's own rhythm — one heavy bar over descending rules — knocked clean out of the fruit's body. No letterform, no chart furniture, no face.

STORY: A visitor sees an apple and understands food. A second look finds the facts panel and understands tracking whose numbers carry sources. The mark promises: what you ate, itemized and confirmed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The glyph sits bare on the page ground at header scale, the apple filling its frame, the label block centered in the body, the leaf at upper right. In tiles (app icon, favicon, touch icon) the same apple sits cream on deep green.
