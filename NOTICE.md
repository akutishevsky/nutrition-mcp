## Production food-data sources

Munch records source identifiers and labels provider data so users can distinguish provider facts, saved snapshots, user-supplied values, published restaurant information, and model-assisted estimates.

### USDA FoodData Central

FoodData Central data are provided by the United States Department of Agriculture. Munch identifies USDA results in user-facing records and retains provider identifiers and source-at-time snapshots where available. USDA data remain subject to the applicable FoodData Central terms and notices.

### Open Food Facts

Open Food Facts database content is made available under the Open Database License. Individual database contents are available under the Database Contents License, and product images may have separate licenses. Munch identifies Open Food Facts results, retains source identifiers, and does not imply that community-provided records are independently verified by Munch.

### Published restaurant information

When a user or ChatGPT supplies nutrition facts from a restaurant's published materials, Munch records them as published restaurant data rather than as USDA or Open Food Facts data. Restaurant names, menus, and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
