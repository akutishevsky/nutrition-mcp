# Household data boundary

A Munch household is a shared workspace for recipes, planned meals, grocery lists, membership display names, roles, and factual activity attribution.

Personal meals, meal items, water logs, weight logs, user-entered goals, personal saved foods, and personal account preferences remain account-scoped. Joining a household does not expose those records to other members.

Shared records retain display-name attribution when a non-owner account is deleted. The deleted account identifier is detached. Household owners must transfer ownership or dissolve the household before deleting their account. Dissolution deletes the shared workspace and its records.

Complete account exports include household records the requesting account can access, but omit other members' email addresses and internal user identifiers.
