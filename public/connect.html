Claude — exact steps

  1. Open Claude and go to Customize → Connectors (in some versions this lives under Settings → Connectors).
  2. Choose +, then Add custom connector.
  3. Enter Name: Munch and the server URL: https://munch.business/mcp, then choose Continue. Leave the Advanced OAuth fields empty — Munch registers itself automatically.
  4. Review the sign-in settings Claude detected, then choose Add.
  5. Choose Connect — your browser opens a Munch page. Enter your email, open the sign-in link Munch sends, and approve the connection.
  6. Return to Claude, turn Munch on in your chat, and ask: “Log my lunch.”

ChatGPT — exact steps

  1. On ChatGPT on the web, open Settings → Apps & Connectors.
  2. Scroll to Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode. (Your AI cannot flip this switch for you — this one is yours.)
  3. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create.
  4. Enter Name: Munch, the MCP server URL: https://munch.business/mcp, and set Authentication to OAuth. Confirm the acknowledgement and choose Create.
  5. Your browser opens a Munch page: enter your email, open the link sent to you, and approve the connection.
  6. Return to ChatGPT, enable Munch in your chat from the + menu, and start logging.

Muse — exact steps

  1. In your terminal, add Munch as a remote MCP server:
    claude mcp add --transport http Munch https://munch.business/mcp
  2. Start Muse and run /mcp, then choose Munch and Authenticate.
  3. Your browser opens a Munch page: enter your email, open the sign-in link, and approve the requested permissions.
  4. Return to Muse: /mcp shows Munch connected, and its 95 tools are available in your session.

Any MCP app — exact steps

  1. Open your app's settings and look for Custom connector, MCP server, or Remote server.
  2. Add a server with Name: Munch.
  3. Paste the Server URL: https://munch.business/mcp.
  4. Choose OAuth for authentication and start the connection.
  5. In the Munch page that opens, enter your email, open the link sent to you, and approve.
  6. Return to your app and confirm Munch shows as connected.

Troubleshooting & permissions

What am I granting?

The approval page lists the exact permissions requested. Approving lets that app call the matching Munch tools for your account only. Munch does not receive your unrelated conversations in that app.

The email link did not arrive

Check spam and filtered folders, confirm the address is typed correctly, then request a fresh link. Links are single-use and expire; a delayed older link may stop working once a newer one is requested. A confirmation tap on the Munch page stops automated email scanners from consuming your link first.

“This request expired”

Authorization requests are intentionally short-lived. Do not reuse the old browser tab — return to your AI app and choose Connect again to start a fresh request. Closing the browser, switching devices mid-flow, reusing a consumed link, or blocking required cookies can also interrupt a connection.

How to revoke access

Open Munch Settings → Connections and revoke the connection you no longer want. Revoking stops that token family immediately; it does not delete your Munch account or your nutrition records.
