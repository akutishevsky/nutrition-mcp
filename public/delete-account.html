Account deletion

Delete your Munch account

Deleting your account is permanent. This page explains how to do it, what deletion removes, and what limited records may remain.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

How to deleteWhat is removedWhat may remainSubscriptions

How to delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, in the app or on the website:

  1. Sign in to Munch with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.

If you want a copy of your data first, generate an account export from Settings before deleting — deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in, email support@munch.business from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will verify the request and process it for you.

What deletion removes

Account deletion removes your active Munch account data, personal nutrition records, saved foods, drafts, preferences, and active connections from the primary application database.

What may remain

Shared display-name attribution in household records may remain, as described in the Privacy Policy. Limited records may also remain temporarily in security logs, database backups until the backup rotation overwrites them, Stripe records, or records Munch must retain for fraud, accounting, legal, or dispute purposes. Crash reports held by Firebase Crashlytics are removed on Firebase's schedule, which begins removal after 90 days.

Deleting your Munch account does not delete conversation history held by your AI provider (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or another MCP client); that history is governed by that provider's own controls.

Premium and subscriptions

Deleting your account ends your Premium access in Munch. A subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store is billed by that store — cancel it in the store as well to stop future charges. If you subscribed on the Munch website, cancel from your account's billing controls before deleting, or contact support@munch.business and we will help.
