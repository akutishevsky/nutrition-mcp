Clear data boundaries

Munch does not train models on your nutrition data.

Munch stores the information needed to provide your account and nutrition tools. It does not sell nutrition data, use it for advertising, or use it to train a Munch AI model.

What Munch controls

Account isolation, nutrition storage, food-source requests, OAuth connections, Stripe entitlement state, exports, deletion, and restricted production access.

What ChatGPT controls

Your conversation is processed by OpenAI before or while ChatGPT calls Munch. OpenAI's retention, training, and privacy choices are governed by your ChatGPT plan and settings—not by Munch.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Munch replace ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT supplies the language, reasoning, photo interpretation, recipe ideas, and planning. Munch supplies persistent nutrition records and callable tools.

Does Munch train on my data?

No. Munch does not train a model on nutrition records. OpenAI separately controls how ChatGPT conversation data is handled according to the user's plan and settings.

Is payment required during the trial?

Stripe collects payment information when the trial starts. The subscription begins charging $4.99 per month after seven days unless canceled.

Is Munch medical advice?

No. Munch is a consumer wellness product. Food data and estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate.
