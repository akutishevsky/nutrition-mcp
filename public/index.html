Questions

What to know before connecting

Does Munch replace ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT handles conversation and reasoning. Munch supplies persistent nutrition data and specialized tools.

Is the free tier permanent?

Yes. Core food search, meal logging, daily summaries, recent history, and account data controls remain available without Premium.

Does Munch calculate what I should eat?

No. Goals are user-entered. Nutrition figures and AI-assisted estimates can be incomplete and are not medical or dietary advice.

What is shared with a household?

Only household recipes, planned meals, groceries, names, roles, and related attribution. Personal meals, water, weight, goals, and saved foods remain private.
