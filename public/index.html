What to know before connecting

How do I connect Munch to my AI?

Copy the block at the top of this page and paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Muse, or any MCP app. Your assistant walks you through adding Munch as a custom connector (server https://munch.business/mcp, sign-in by OAuth). Sign-in is an email link — if you are new, that link creates your account. Prefer tapping it in yourself? The step-by-step instructions cover each app.

Does Munch replace my AI assistant?

No. Your assistant handles conversation and reasoning. Munch supplies the persistent nutrition data and the specialized tools.

Is the free tier permanent?

Yes. Core food search, meal logging, daily summaries, recent history, and account data controls stay available without Premium.

Does Munch calculate what I should eat?

No. Goals are user-entered. Nutrition figures and AI-assisted estimates can be incomplete and are not medical or dietary advice.

What is shared with a household?

Recipes, planned meals, groceries, names, roles, and related attribution. Personal meals, water, weight, goals, and saved foods remain private.
