Privacy Policy

Munch is a consumer nutrition service designed to work through ChatGPT and compatible MCP clients.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Summary

Munch does not sell nutrition data, use Google Analytics or advertising trackers, or train a Munch AI model on your nutrition records. ChatGPT is operated by OpenAI and is governed separately by OpenAI's terms, privacy policy, plan, and user settings.

What Munch stores

Munch stores the information needed to provide the service, including:

  • Account email, login challenges, sessions, and security records.
  • Meals, meal items, calories, nutrients, meal times, notes, sources, assumptions, drafts, and confirmations.
  • Saved foods, usual meals, food-source identifiers, and barcode results you choose to save.
  • Water, weight, goals, timezone, units, and other account preferences.
  • OAuth clients, authorization sessions, token families, connection status, and revocation records.
  • Stripe customer, subscription, entitlement, trial, renewal, cancellation, and payment-status identifiers. Munch does not store raw card numbers.
  • Exports, deletion requests, bounded operational logs, and security telemetry needed to run and protect the service.

Tool inputs

When ChatGPT calls a Munch tool, Munch receives the tool arguments needed to perform the request. Those arguments can contain food descriptions, quantities, dates, nutrition values, water, weight, goals, or account instructions. Munch does not automatically receive unrelated parts of your conversation.

Why it is stored

Munch uses this information to authenticate you, maintain persistent nutrition history, search food databases, answer authorized tool requests, enforce subscription access, prevent abuse, support export and deletion, and operate the service.

ChatGPT and OpenAI are separate from Munch

Your conversation is processed by ChatGPT before or while ChatGPT decides whether to call Munch. OpenAI—not Munch—controls how ChatGPT conversation content is retained, reviewed, or used under the applicable ChatGPT plan, account settings, and OpenAI policies.

Munch cannot promise that OpenAI will not retain or use conversation content. Users should review OpenAI's current privacy controls and avoid entering information they do not want processed by ChatGPT.

Munch does not train a separate model on nutrition records. Munch may use aggregate operational information that excludes nutrition-record content to identify failures, latency, abuse, or capacity needs.

Service providers and external data sources

  • Railway hosts the Munch application and Railway PostgreSQL database.
  • Stripe processes checkout, recurring billing, payment methods, invoices, cancellations, and subscription recovery.
  • Email delivery provider processes the email address and secure sign-in link needed for passwordless login.
  • USDA FoodData Central receives food-search requests when Munch searches USDA data.
  • Open Food Facts receives food or barcode lookup requests when that source is used.
  • OpenAI/ChatGPT processes the conversation and decides what tool arguments to send to Munch.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Munch does not use Google Analytics and does not operate an advertising profile.

Security and database protections

Munch uses HTTPS in production, signed and expiring sessions, OAuth authorization with PKCE, verified Stripe webhook signatures, rate limiting, bounded request bodies, and account-scoped database access patterns. Nutrition records are associated with an account and are not exposed through routine billing or support views.

Access to production systems is restricted, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Munch does not claim zero-knowledge storage, end-to-end encryption, or that an authorized operator could never access the database during security, legal, backup, or incident-response work.

Export, connections, and deletion

You can export meal history, revoke individual ChatGPT/MCP connections, manage billing through Stripe, and permanently delete your Munch account through the account portal.

Account deletion removes active Munch account data, nutrition records, saved foods, drafts, preferences, and active connections from the primary application database. Some limited records may remain temporarily in security logs, database backups, Stripe records, or records Munch must retain for fraud, accounting, legal, or dispute purposes.

Deleting Munch data does not delete conversation history held by ChatGPT or OpenAI.

Retention

Active account data is retained while the account is open and as needed to provide the service. Expired login challenges, sessions, OAuth artifacts, exports, and operational records are subject to bounded cleanup. Backups and legally required billing records can persist after deletion for a limited period.

Consumer wellness product

Munch is not an electronic health record, medical provider, emergency service, or HIPAA-covered clinical service. Nutrition data, database records, and AI-assisted estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate and are not medical advice.

Policy changes

This policy may change as Munch, its providers, or legal requirements change. Material updates will be reflected by a revised date and, where appropriate, an in-product notice.

Contact

A dedicated support and privacy contact will be published before general availability. Until then, project issues may be reported through the Munch GitHub repository without including sensitive nutrition or account data.
