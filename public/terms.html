1. The Munch service

Munch is a consumer wellness service that gives ChatGPT and compatible MCP clients authorized tools for food search, nutrition records, saved foods, recipes, meal calendars, grocery lists, household collaboration, water and weight tracking, and factual summaries.

Munch is not a standalone AI model and does not control ChatGPT. OpenAI's products, plans, policies, availability, and model behavior are separate from Munch.

2. Free access, Premium, trial, and cancellation

Munch Free provides permanent core access without a payment method, subject to the limits shown on the Munch website. Connecting Munch to ChatGPT does not require Premium.

Munch Premium is purchased independently on the Munch website. The current offer is a 30-day trial followed by $4.99 per month, plus applicable taxes. Payment information is collected by Stripe when the Premium trial begins. Trial availability may be limited to eligible accounts.

Unless canceled before the trial ends, Premium automatically converts to a paid monthly subscription and renews each month until canceled. You authorize Stripe to charge the saved payment method for recurring fees and applicable taxes.

Payment methods, invoices, and cancellation are managed through the Stripe-hosted customer portal linked from the Munch account portal. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid period unless Stripe or applicable law provides otherwise. Canceling Premium does not delete the Munch account or personal data; the account returns to applicable Free capabilities.

Promotions, refunds, credits, and trial eligibility may be limited. Except where required by law or expressly stated, fees already charged are non-refundable.

3. Household workspaces

A directly eligible Premium account may create and own one household subject to the current member limit. The owner can invite accounts as members or viewers. Members may edit shared records while viewers are read-only. Household access depends on the owner's applicable entitlement.

Household recipes, planned meals, grocery lists, display names, roles, and factual activity attribution are visible to active household members. Personal meals, water, weight, personal saved foods, and personal goals remain private unless the user deliberately creates a record in the shared household workspace.

Household members are responsible for inviting only people they are authorized to include and for the information they place in the shared workspace. A household owner must transfer ownership or dissolve the household before deleting the owner's account. Dissolution permanently deletes shared household records. A former member's display-name attribution may remain with shared activity as described in the Privacy Policy.
